Reflections

This project greatly enhanced my understanding of CI and CD processes, clearly showing the difference between continuous integration and continuous deployment. It connected many parts of the course that I had not fully grasped in isolation.

The most challenging part was deploying the Kubernetes cluster, particularly the kubeadm installation and configuring its ecosystem with kubectl and dependencies. Understanding Ingress definitions also took time, as I initially thought each microservice required a separate ingress.

Implementing GitOps with ArgoCD was challenging but valuable. Learning how ArgoCD operates, its role in automating deployments, and its interaction with the cluster helped me appreciate the benefits of GitOps compared to manual deployment.

Overall, the project successfully unified the tools and topics we studied throughout the year, including AWS for infrastructure and Terraform for environment deployment, giving me practical experience in orchestrating a complete automated deployment pipeline.
